Jon Wonders Studio

The desktop studio behind the Jon Wonders YouTube channel

Jon Wonders Studio is a private desktop app that runs on the channel owner's own computer. It is used to make the animated explainer videos and short videos of the Jon Wonders YouTube channel.

What it does with TikTok

When a short video is finished, the app can send it to the channel's own TikTok account as a draft, using TikTok's Content Posting API (upload to inbox). TikTok then shows a notification in the TikTok app. The account owner opens it, adds the caption and decides whether to post it. The app never posts anything publicly by itself, and it is only used with the channel's own TikTok account.

What it uses

TikTok Login Kit with two permissions: user.info.basic (the account's display name, to show which account is connected) and video.upload (to send a video to that account's TikTok inbox as a draft).

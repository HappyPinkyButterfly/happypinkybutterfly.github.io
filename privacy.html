Privacy Policy

Effective 9 November 2026

This policy explains what Jon Wonders Studio ("the app") does with information when it is connected to a TikTok account. The app is a personal desktop tool. It runs only on the computer of the owner of the Jon Wonders YouTube channel and is used only with the channel's own TikTok account.

Information the app receives from TikTok

When the account owner signs in with TikTok and clicks Authorize, TikTok gives the app:

  • basic account information (open ID and display name, scope user.info.basic), used only to show which account is connected;
  • an access token and a refresh token that let the app send videos to that account's TikTok inbox (scope video.upload).

How it is used

The app uses this information only to send videos the owner made to the owner's own TikTok inbox as drafts, and to show the upload status. The app posts nothing publicly; the owner finishes and posts each video in the TikTok app.

Where it is stored

The tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer, in its user settings. The app has no server, no database, no analytics and no advertising. This website uses no cookies and no tracking.

Sharing

The information is never sold or shared, and it is never sent anywhere other than to TikTok's own API.

Videos

Videos are sent straight from the owner's computer to TikTok. The app keeps only a local log of which file was sent and when.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time in the TikTok app, under Settings and privacy, in the list of apps connected to the account. Removing the app's access stops all uploads. The tokens on the owner's computer can be deleted at any time.

Children

The app is not meant for anyone other than the account owner and does not collect information from anyone else.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new effective date.
